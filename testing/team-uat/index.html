The rule

Respond like yourself. The system has to work with what you give it. Be creative.

Choose any chip, type your own question, challenge the premise, or redirect the conversation within the journey.

Before every journey

Reset first. Then behave naturally.

01

Reset

Click the Algolia Assistant logo. Confirm it rotates and clears only Algolia-owned journey, greeting, and chat state.

02

Browse

Follow the stated route. Keep the tab visible, dwell for the required time, and reach the required depth.

03

Engage

Do not open chat yourself. When aRRIe appears, answer however you want: chip, free text, challenge, or redirect.

04

Verify

Check that aRRIe remembers the journey, answers your actual response with evidence, and asks a useful next question.

A popup alone is not a pass. The experience must recognize intent, earn engagement, carry context forward, and help the visitor take a meaningful next step.

Testing a technical specialist flow: product page to product documentation.

Run these as two separate tests. Reset the Assistant before starting each one.

  1. Reset the Assistant.
  2. Open AI Search and stay for 5+ seconds.

  4. Respond naturally when the greeting appears.

T2

Product to documentation

Click path Products → AI Search → Read the AI Search docs

  1. Reset the Assistant.
  2. Open AI Search; stay 5+ sec and scroll 25%+.
  3. Let the product greeting appear, but leave that greeting unanswered.
  4. Open the multilingual FAQ and click Read the AI Search docs.
  5. On documentation: stay 5+ sec, scroll 25%+, and wait for the destination greeting to replace the first one.
  6. Only then respond naturally.
Look for

A greeting that connects the AI Search product you explored with the InstantSearch documentation you are now reading, then handles your technical question intelligently.

Tester response: any relevant chip or free text. Ask, challenge, or redirect naturally. There is no required question.

Evidence collection

Turn every journey into evidence executives can trust.

For every scenario, capture:

  • Scenario and pages visited
  • Pass, needs work, or blocked
  • Start, CTA, destination, greeting, and answer
  • The response you actually gave
  • When the greeting appeared
  • Whether it remembered the journey
  • Handoff or specialist trace ID
  • Sources and any questionable claim

For a failure

Expected vs. actual

Write one clean sentence for each. Reset and repeat once. Mark intermittent behavior explicitly instead of smoothing it over.

Example

Expected product-to-pricing context. Actual greeting discussed pricing generically and omitted the product after two fresh attempts.
