# Team Demo and UAT Briefing - Design Thinking

## Mental Model

This is a presentation, not a test-management application. Arijit moves through eight slides while recording the team briefing. The team receives a story, exact journey steps, expected behavior, and evidence rules; testers remain free to respond naturally.

## Information Architecture

| Slide | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 1 | Testing thesis and tester-autonomy rule |
| 2 | Reset and complete testing method |
| 3 | Technical journey: T1 and T2 |
| 4 | Business journey: B1 and B2 |
| 5 | Commercial journey: C1 and C2 |
| 6 | Specialist journey: S1 and S2 |
| 7 | Evidence and issue reporting |
| 8 | Completion criteria and canonical guide |

## Interaction Flow

1. Open the HTML deck locally.
2. Move slide by slide using keyboard or visible arrow controls.
3. Explain the two scenarios on each category slide.
4. Reinforce that testers choose their own chip or free-text response.
5. Direct testers to the canonical guide for full acceptance criteria.

## Cognitive Load

Only one slide is visible at a time. Each category presents exactly two scenarios in parallel. There are no forms, checkboxes, assignments, scoring matrices, or prescribed tester questions.

## Emotional Journey

Understand the thesis -> learn the method -> see four coherent stories -> know what evidence matters -> leave with a clear definition of done.

## Pre-Mortem

- **Popup mistaken for a pass:** every category names the downstream handoff, routing, grounding, and discovery checks.
- **Tester follows a script:** autonomy language appears in the opening, method, and every category.
- **Failure lacks evidence:** the evidence slide requires expected versus actual and one fresh retry.
- **Deck becomes a dense manual:** detail remains in the canonical Markdown; slides retain the speaking spine.
- **Recording becomes awkward:** fixed slide count, keyboard navigation, section name, and counter keep the walkthrough controlled.

## Aesthetic

Algolia blue, white, cool lavender, dark ink, DM Sans, and restrained shadows. The deck uses a compact internal-product tone rather than marketing-page composition.
