Opening frame

The Ask AI contrast

The room already knows Ask AI. We do not compete by saying "we built another Ask AI." We compete by showing a bigger use case for Algolia's existing strengths.

Question Ask AI product posture Content Engagement posture
When does engagement start? After the visitor decides to ask a question. When consented behavior shows meaningful page intent or a supported journey progression.
What does the system know? The user's explicit question and retrieved content. The question, page category, journey context, source page, destination page, dwell, scroll, and business moment.
What is the business motion? Answer and educate. Answer, guide, qualify, route, and move the visitor toward proof, evaluation, POC, or sales conversation.
Presenter line

"Ask AI is powerful when someone raises their hand. Content Engagement is what happens when the website can tell the visitor is almost ready to raise their hand, then starts the right grounded conversation."

Showcase paths

Step-by-step script

Each use case answers four questions for the presenter: what are we showing, who are we showing it to, what are we clicking, and which screens are we pulling.

Use case 1 - 1:00 to 3:00

On-page intent recognition

A visitor dwells at least five seconds and scrolls at least 25 percent on a Product page or a Solution page. The system recognizes meaningful on-page intent and opens a personalized proactive greeting.

Canonical singleton capability Product: /products/ai-search Solution: /industries/grocery 5s dwell + 25% scroll

Value shown

More engagement from visitors who are showing intent but have not typed a question, opened chat, or clicked a CTA yet.

1What we show

Product-only and Solution-only are two examples of one broader on-page capability, not separate magic tricks.

2Who sees it

Stephen, Piyush, product leadership, marketing, revenue leadership, and the delivery team.

3What we click

  • Reset once with the Algolia Assistant logo
  • Open /products/ai-search or /industries/grocery
  • Keep the tab visible for five seconds
  • Scroll beyond 25 percent
  • Wait for the proactive greeting

4Expected behavior

A page-specific greeting appears after both thresholds are met and offers useful directions without mentioning tracking.

Presenter line

"Before we move across pages, start with the simplest signal: the visitor is leaning into this page. The system sees enough intent to help, but it does not need to pretend it knows more than it does."

Recovery paths

Backup script for demo risk

The CEO demo should feel live, but it should not depend on luck. Each risk has a planned recovery that still tells the Content Engagement story.

On-page greeting does not trigger

Reset once, keep the tab visible, wait five seconds, and confirm 25 percent scroll. If it still fails, use the verified page links to show the intended path and mark the trigger as needing live investigation.

H-E-B path does not fire

Confirm the direct Grocery -> H-E-B route was used and the source greeting stayed unanswered. Do not switch to a different customer story unless the page exposes that exact link.

Enterprise pricing path does not fire

Confirm the real "Learn more about pricing" link was clicked from Enterprise Search. Product -> Pricing remains supported, but do not present it as tonight's canonical commercial-buyer path.

Latency is high

Keep narrating the state transition: tracker, compiler, controller, greeting request, grounded handoff. Then use the screen pull while the live answer catches up.

Answer lacks proof

Ask a narrower follow-up tied to the current page, then show source tiles or trace evidence. Do not invent case studies, ROI, conversion metrics, or pricing details.
