Simple demo script

Two ways Content Engagement helps a visitor move forward

30-second read

Reactive flow The visitor asks. Algolia answers with TL/DR, details, links, docs, and follow-up questions.
Proactive flow The visitor shows intent. Algolia opens a personalized path to proof or pricing.
Half 1

Reactive flow

The visitor asks a question directly. The demo should show that the answer is useful, grounded, and easy to continue.

Question 1

Business user question

"How can a non-technical marketing team improve search results day to day without waiting on developers, and what business outcomes should leadership watch?"

Expected story: answer starts with a TL/DR, explains how business teams can act, connects that control to outcomes without inventing ROI, includes inline links, and asks one useful follow-up.

Question 2

Technical user question

"We have an ERP, PIM, and CMS already. Is Algolia API-first enough to add enterprise search without replatforming, and what should the first POC prove?"

Expected story: answer explains architecture fit in plain English, links to docs or implementation material, separates integration risk from implementation effort, and asks one focused follow-up.

Half 2

Proactive flow

The visitor does not ask first. The site recognizes behavior and opens a relevant conversation.

Technical user

Product to proof

Product page -> technical proof, docs, or implementation evidence

  1. Visitor studies a product page.
  2. System recognizes technical evaluation intent.
  3. Greeting offers implementation proof and docs.
Business user

Solution to proof

Solution page -> customer proof

  1. Visitor studies a solution page.
  2. Visitor moves to a customer story.
  3. Greeting connects the business problem to proof.
Commercial user

Product or solution to pricing

Product or solution page -> pricing

  1. Visitor studies product or solution value.
  2. Visitor moves to pricing.
  3. Greeting frames cost, value, effort, and next step.
