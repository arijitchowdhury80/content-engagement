## Unresolved Experience Decision

The current UAT instructions expect an unanswered source greeting to be replaced by a destination transition greeting. The latest navigation policy says no proactive greeting may interrupt an active greeting or conversation. Both cannot remain true.

Recommended policy, pending explicit founder approval:

1. An unanswered proactive greeting may be superseded by a higher-value greeting after an immediate supported transition.
2. Once the visitor responds, the experience is an engaged conversation and must never be interrupted by another proactive greeting.
3. Back, Forward, Reload, stale history, intervening pages, and unsupported edges never cause replacement.

Do not rewrite the UAT deck around this recommendation until it is approved.
