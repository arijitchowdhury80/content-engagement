## The DoD constraint that shapes everything

**Verbatim quotes. Summaries fail the gate.** The entire point is language Arijit can
quote back to a CMO. "Sales said buyers struggle to find pricing" is worthless.
"Every eval, the first thing they ask me for is a pricing page I can send them, and I
never have one that answers their question" is the asset. Record or transcribe.

## Method

**`[WU-14.1]` Premise check — do this first, it is cheap.**
Three questions to 3–5 people:
- When a prospect is evaluating us, what do they most often fail to find on algolia.com?
- What page or link do you send most often, and why that one?
- If the site's homepage were a search box, what would break for you?

**`[WU-14.2]` Interviews — 2 to 3 people, 30 min each.**
Cover: common buyer paths · objections heard repeatedly · what buyers need in discovery ·
which pages they actually send · what they wish existed · where the current nav fails them.
Ask for stories, not opinions: *"tell me about the last deal where the website helped or
hurt"* beats *"is our website good."*

**`[WU-14.3]` Win/loss review.** Win/loss notes, call snippets, discovery summaries if
accessible. Pull language about evaluation and information-finding specifically.

**Then:** tag every quote with which of the 8 journeys it touches, so WU-19 and WU-26
can pull evidence per journey rather than re-reading the whole file.
