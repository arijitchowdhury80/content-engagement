## Do not reuse AC2_WWW_MULTI_NEURAL as the demo index

Measured 2026-08-05, aggregate facet counts not sampling:
- 7914 of 8353 records are `nonprod20260220`. **Only 122 are prod.**
- 4177 of 6178 tagged records are `Doc`. Only 170 are `Website`.
- `facets.facet0-5` are 0.6–14% populated and hold blog/docs taxonomy
  (blog category, vertical, framework, language, integration, "supported").
  **They are not the six axes** despite looking like six slots.

**Copy its configuration pattern, not its data:**
- `mode: "neuralSearch"` — neural is proven available on this app
- `searchableAttributes`: `["title", "unordered(abstract)", "unordered(description)",
  "unordered(tags)", "unordered(keywords)", "unordered(category)", "unordered(url)"]`
- `afterDistinct()` faceting, `searchable()` on facets that need typeahead
- body-in-a-replica pattern (`AC2_WWW_MULTI_NEURAL_body`) — keeps the main index small
  while still allowing full-text depth

## Method

1. Pick an index name that cannot be confused with anything on this shared 146-index
   account. Suggested: `SEARCHFIRST_WWW_v1`. State it in the Asana comment.
2. Join corpus records to their six-axis classification on `url`. **Report the join rate.**
   An unstated join rate hides silent record loss.
3. Transform to `index-schema.json`. Set `objectID` deterministically from the URL so
   re-indexing is idempotent rather than duplicating.
4. Decide the body strategy: full body in a replica, or truncated in the main index.
   55.6 MB total, median 23.7k chars/page — some records will exceed Algolia's record size
   limit. **Split or truncate deliberately and record which.** Silent truncation is the
   failure that produces a "why does search miss this content" bug later.
5. Push records. Configure settings: searchable attributes, the six facets, custom ranking,
   `mode: neuralSearch`.
6. Exclude junk from the demo index: `is404`-style dead pages, the 3 thin-body records,
   `utility` page types if they add nothing. **Say what was excluded and why.**
7. **Verify with real queries, not a record count.** Run the navigational queries the demo
   depends on — "pricing", "shopify", "how do I integrate", plus one per journey — and
   confirm the right page comes back first.
